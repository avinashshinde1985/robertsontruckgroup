How to measure a wheelbase

5 minutesTape measureSingle or tandem axle

Your wheelbase and cab-to-axle decide which body lengths fit your truck. Measure once, write it on the wheelbase sheet, and send it with your quote request.

  1. Park on level ground with the front wheels pointed straight.
  2. Wheelbase (WB): center of the front axle to the center of the rear axle. On a single axle, measure to the middle of the rear hub.On a tandem, measure to the midpoint between the two rear axles.
  3. Cab-to-axle (CA):Cab-to-tandem (CT): back of the cab to the center of the rear axle midpoint. This is the number that decides body length.
  4. Frame behind cab: back of the cab to the end of the frame rails.
  5. Read GVWR and axle ratings off the sticker inside the driver's door, and take a photo of it.

Tip: measure to the back of the cab itself, not to exhaust stacks, a headache rack or tanks. Note anything mounted behind the cab, because it can change what fits.

Measurements that matter

3 minutesPlain English

Having the right equipment on the back of your truck is key to any job. These are the numbers we check before matching a body to a chassis.

GVWRGross vehicle weight rating

The most the truck can weigh fully loaded: chassis, body, fuel, crew and cargo. Over 26,000 lb needs a CDL.

PayloadWhat you can actually haul

GVWR minus the weight of the truck with its body. A heavier body leaves less room for cargo.

GAWRGross axle weight rating

The most each axle can carry, front and rear listed separately on the door sticker.

WBWheelbase

Center of the front axle to the center of the rear axle, or the tandem midpoint.

CACab-to-axle

Back of the cab to the center of the rear axle. The key number for choosing body length.

CTCab-to-tandem

On a tandem truck, back of the cab to the midpoint (trunnion) between the rear axles.

TanksFuel & DEF tank location

Tanks on the frame rails or behind the cab can block a hoist, toolboxes or body mounts. Tell us where yours are.
