What We Do

From 3 trucks to well over 200 on the lot

Robertson Truck Sales was founded in 1984 by Phil Robertson in Mount Vernon, Ohio. With only 3 trucks in inventory, Robertson Truck Sales quickly grew over the years to well over 200 trucks on the lot.

Specializing in body upfitting and fabrication, Robertson Truck Sales provides its clients with the highest quality medium & heavy-duty trucks in the industry today.

Our Journey

Four decades of building trucks & trust

  1. 1984

    Founded

    Phil Robertson opens Robertson Truck Sales in Mount Vernon, Ohio — with only 3 trucks in inventory.

  2. Growth

    200+ trucks

    Specializing in body upfitting and fabrication, the lot grows over the years to well over 200 trucks.
