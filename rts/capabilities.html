Measurements Matter

The right body on the right chassis

Having the right piece of equipment on the back of your truck is key to any job, large or small. Many factors are important in order to establish the capability between a body and a truck.

  • Gross vehicle weight rating
  • Payload
  • Gross axle weight rating
  • Wheelbase
  • Fuel & DEF tank location

Most importantly, understand how the cab to axle (or trunnion) and wheelbase measurement comes into play. Tap a dimension to learn more.

Get a Quote to Install Wheelbase sheet (PDF)

Cab-to-axle (CA) dimension

The distance from the back of the cab to the centerline of the rear axle, this dimension helps determine the length of body that can be mounted on the chassis.
