Wheelbase Measurement Sheet

How to measure

  1. Park on level ground with the front wheels straight.
  2. Wheelbase (WB): from the center of the front axle to the center of the rear axle. On a tandem, measure to the midpoint between the two rear axles.
  3. Cab-to-axle (CA): from the back of the cab to the center of the rear axle (or tandem midpoint).
  4. Frame behind cab: from the back of the cab to the end of the frame rails.
  5. Find GVWR and axle ratings on the sticker inside the driver's door.
