Buyer's Guide

Brands and Things to Check When Buying a Used Truck in Ohio

There are many things to check when buying a used truck. There are many advantages to getting a used truck such as saving money on your purchase. However, you need to make sure you’re getting a good deal before you buy a used truck in Ohio. It is the point of this article to help you find the diamond in the rough quickly by giving you the 3 top things to check when buying a used truck.

01

Inspect the Body

You must know what to look for when buying a used truck. Rust is one of the first things to check for when buying a used truck.

Read more

Of course, many second-hand vehicles may have rust, but it’s the location that matters. There is significant damage to a truck if there is rust on the exhaust pipes, muffler, wheels, frame rails, or floor pans.

You may notice certain parts of the body seem newer than others. This could be a sign that there was some serious damage to the truck that caused the owner to replace parts.

Scratches and dents should be at the top of things to check when buying a used truck. You need a keen eye for subtle cosmetic damage.

02

Rev The Engine

One of the most important things to check when buying a used truck is the engine. Make sure the engine is cool before you take a look at it.

Read more

Check the ground under the hood for splattered oil. This is a major red flag. Another thing to check before you buy a used truck in Ohio is the battery.

If you see any corrosion on it, you’ll probably want to keep shopping.

Check the color of the coolant in the radiator — it should be green or orange.

Radiator leaks are also on the list of things to check when buying a used truck. Stains on the outside of the radiator could mean there are leaks.

If you want to buy a used truck in Ohio, make sure the truck you’re looking at has a good battery. There are indicators that will help you.

Green is usually a sign of a good battery, while yellow or black could mean the opposite.

Have a mechanic take a look at it if possible. They can do a “load” test. A mechanic can be of great help with all the things to check when buying a used truck.

03

Service Records

If you really want to buy a used truck in Ohio, then check the truck’s history. Every car has a paper trail, so ordering a complete report is highly advised.

Read more

Repairs and accidents will be easy to spot. However, there may be many more subtle issues that turn out to be deal-breakers.

Finding out the number of previous owners should be high on the list of things to check when buying a used truck. If there have been many over a short period of time, you don’t want it.

You want your used truck to be up-to-date and able to pass emissions testing. This will make sure you are in line with any safety regulations in your area.

The body, engine, and service records are the top 3 things to check when buying a used truck.
